How It Works

High-Fidelity Real-Time AR Tracking

Our Virtual Try-On engine does not simply paint static elements onto a 2D image. It calculates a complete 3D spatial orientation of your face structure in real time.

  • Nose Bridge Lock (Landmark 168): Places the 3D model with precision at the anatomical resting point.
  • Dynamic Pupil Scaling: Measures outer-eye separation to perfectly scale the temple arms and frame size based on proximity to camera.
  • Euler Rotational Matrix: Calculates Roll, Yaw, and Pitch angles instantly, maintaining tracking lock even as you tilt or turn your head.
  • Refractive Physics Shaders: Simulates glass index of refraction (1.52) to authentically refract background lighting.
